## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural distinction, quiet opulence, and modern hospitality tailored for ultra-luxury residential living in Bengaluru. Drawing inspiration from contemporary organic architecture, sculpted bronze facades, warm stone, and refined biophilic living, the visual style fuses Warm Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Elegance.

The audience encompasses C-suite executives, global expatriates, and high-net-worth individuals seeking a bespoke sanctuary in the tech capital of India. The experience prioritizes poise over noise: generous proportions, structured symmetry, soft tactile surfaces, subtle metallic sheen, and an unhurried browsing journey that elevates residential leasing into a curated private acquisition.

## Colors

The color palette captures warm ivory architectural planes, brushed bronze and champagne joinery, and grounded charcoal masonry:

- **Primary Accent (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne gold used for primary call-to-action treatments, active state borders, delicate architectural dividers, and key property badges. A brighter tier (`#D4AF37`) functions as an interactive hover state and badge highlight.
- **Secondary / Ink (`#1A1918` & `#2B2927`)**: Deep obsidian charcoal and slate stone used for authoritative editorial typography, high-contrast dark buttons, and structure headers. Avoid pure pitch `#000000`.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#8A6D4B`)**: Deep patinated bronze reflecting the architectural facade detailing, ideal for tertiary actions, icon outlines, and secondary tag containers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5`)**: Pure warm ivory providing an ambient, non-glare foundation. Layered cards utilize `#F4EFEA` (warm sandstone beige) paired with structural hairline borders in `#E8E2D9` (subtle warm grey).

## Typography

The pairing blends the timeless grace of **EB Garamond** with the precision and legibility of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Display (EB Garamond)**: Reserved for hero statements, residence titles, floor plan names, and architectural pull quotes. Its organic curves reflect classical heritage and contemporary elegance.
- **Systematic & Informational (Manrope)**: Delivers exceptional clarity across property specifications, pricing figures, layout dimensions, amenities matrices, and high-conversion interactive forms.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase styling with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror upscale editorial catalog standards.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid provides rhythm across viewport scales, rooted in architectural golden ratios and deliberate whitespace.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `5rem` page margins. Hero layouts and unit showcases take advantage of asymmetric 7/5 or 8/4 grid compositions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Cards re-flow to dual-column representations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Full-width horizontal swipe rails allow seamless inspection of residence floorplans and amenity galleries.
- **Section Spacing**: Generous `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps separate key content bands, preventing the visual clutter typical of mass-market portals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic harsh drop shadows, utilizing warm architectural lighting and layered materiality instead:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas `#FAF8F5` hosts elevated cards rendered in warm sandstone `#F4EFEA`. Modal overlays and floating inquiry sheets use `#FFFFFF` with warm undertones.
- **Hairline Architectural Outlines**: Low-contrast borders (`#E8E2D9` at 1px thickness) define containers, image frames, and modular panels with razor-thin craftsmanship.
- **Ambient Warm Shadows**: Elevation employs multi-stop, highly diffused shadows with warm umber undertones (`rgba(43, 41, 39, 0.04)` to `rgba(43, 41, 39, 0.08)` blur radii from 12px to 32px), creating the illusion of natural overhead gallery illumination rather than digital depth.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses subtle soft radii (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` for cards, `0.75rem` for modals), honoring the sculpted bronze lintels, vertical louvers, and crisp mullions of the residence architecture. While the overall profile stays tailored and orthogonal, small micro-radii soften touch interactions without lapsing into casual roundness.

## Components

### Buttons & Real Estate CTAs
- **Primary Inquiry CTA**: High-contrast `#1A1918` background, `#FAF8F5` text, and subtle champagne gold (`#C5A880`) bottom border or hover accent. Generous padding (`0.875rem` vertical, `2rem` horizontal), uppercase tracking (`label-lg`), with transition to `#C5A880` background on hover.
- **VIP Schedule Private Viewing**: Sandstone `#C5A880` background, rich charcoal `#1A1918` typography, subtle inset ambient glow.
- **Secondary Action**: Border button with 1px `#E8E2D9` border, warm ivory surface, transitioning to rich charcoal on hover.

### Property Showcase Cards
- Encased in `#F4EFEA` sandstone beige with 1px `#E8E2D9` border. Aspect ratios for imagery respect 16:10 or 4:3 architectural framing.
- Price labels set in `headline-sm` (`EB Garamond`) coupled with uppercase currency and frequency tags in `label-sm` (`Manrope`).
- Subtle bronze hover lift (transform `translateY(-4px)` with soft warm shadow diffusion).

### Filter Chips & Residence Selectors
- Unselected: Crisp transparent surface with 1px `#E8E2D9` border, `#2B2927` text.
- Selected: `#1A1918` fill with crisp white text, or `#C5A880` with dark bronze type. Pill geometry avoided; uses rounded soft corners (`4px`).

### Input Fields & Viewing Request Forms
- Surfaces rendered in `#FFFFFF` with 1px hairline border (`#E8E2D9`).
- Focus state brings a 1px solid `#C5A880` stroke and warm halo glow (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)`).
- Labels float in uppercase `label-md` tracking above inputs.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px forms with `2px` soft radius; unselected states use `#E8E2D9` strokes.
- Checked states fill with `#1A1918` displaying a champagne gold checkmark icon.

### Architectural Floor Plan & Amenity Viewers
- Full-bleed viewing frames bordered in subtle warm grey `#E8E2D9`, featuring floating high-contrast zoom controllers and curated specification trays with Manrope tabular figures.
